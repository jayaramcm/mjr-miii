Privacy policy

MJR Infra Developers · Last updated 4 November 2026

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, request a price sheet or documents, or book a site visit on this website, we collect your name, your mobile number, the reason for your enquiry, any plot number or preferred visit date you choose, and the time you sent it.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your request: to call or message you about MJR Township, share prices, documents and availability, and arrange site visits. We do not sell your details or share them with third parties for their own marketing.

Who can see it

Your details are available to the MJR Infra Developers sales team and to the service providers who host this website and deliver enquiries to our team, only for that purpose.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond to you and follow up on your interest in the project, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to see, correct or delete your details, at any time. Write to sales@mjrinfra.com or call +91 90100 55596.
