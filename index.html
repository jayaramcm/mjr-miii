          Frequently asked questions

          QWhere is MJR Township?

          At Survey No. 720 and 729, on the Tirupati Highway (NH 167K), Kalwakurthy, Nagarkurnool district, Telangana 509 324. See the map and directions.

          QHow many plots are there, and what sizes?

          There are 104 residential plots across 7.93 acres, from approximately 120 to 550 square yards. See which plots are available.

          QIs it a gated community?

          Yes. MJR Township is a gated layout with 30 ft and 40 ft internal roads and a 150 ft abutting road.

          QIs the project RERA registered?

          Yes. It is registered under TG RERA with registration number P01600010465. You can verify it on the TG RERA portal.

          QWhat amenities are planned, and what is ready?

          A swimming pool, children’s play area, senior citizen park, multipurpose lawn, outdoor fitness area, three parks and an EV charging station. The amenities list shows the status of each.

          QWhat is the price of a plot?

          Prices depend on the plot’s size, facing and position. and our team will share prices and payment terms.

          QCan I visit the site before deciding?

          Of course. and our team will show you the plots, the roads and the layout in person.
